01

Vision

People rarely know exactly who to buy from — they know what they need. Every existing marketplace still starts from supply: scroll a feed, filter a list, hope the right seller shows up. Servuber starts from demand.

"Post what you need, set your budget and deadline, and let businesses compete for your order."

— ONE-LINE PITCH
Today

Search-first marketplaces

Uber finds a driver, Airbnb finds a stay, Fiverr finds a freelancer — each optimised for one vertical, each still asking the buyer to search.

Servuber

Demand-first marketplace

One request format works across services, products and rentals. The buyer describes the outcome; the category and channel are inferred.

Outcome

Providers compete, not buyers

Businesses spend their effort winning a job that already exists, instead of buyers spending effort finding a business that might say yes.

02

Users & roles

Four roles share one request object, each seeing a different face of it.

Guest

Browses public requests and category activity before creating an account. No posting, no offers.

Buyer

Homeowners, renters, students, families, event organisers and businesses. Create requests, compare offers, pay, review.

Provider

Tradespeople, retailers, freelancers, agencies, manufacturers. Maintain a business profile, submit offers, fulfil and invoice.

Admin

Verification, dispute resolution, payments oversight, fraud and content moderation, platform reporting.

06

AI layer

AI sits at three points: turning a vague need into a postable request, helping providers respond fast, and keeping the marketplace honest.

Concierge

Request writer

"Need a plumber" becomes a complete, licensed-trade-ready brief with scope, tools required and deadline — extracted from a single sentence or voice note.

Concierge

Budget estimator

Suggests a realistic price range when the buyer doesn't know one, using category and recent comparable requests.

Trust

Scam & anomaly detection

Flags suspiciously low quotes, duplicated portfolios, and unverifiable documents before a buyer ever sees the offer.

Matching

Best-match ranking

Ranks incoming offers on price, distance, rating, response time, completion rate and the buyer's past preferences.

Provider

Proposal & pricing assist

Drafts an offer and suggests a competitive price the moment a matching request appears.

Support

Chat assistant

Drafts responses to buyer questions in-thread — e.g. explaining a price difference — for the provider to approve and send.

Trust & payments

Every dollar sits in escrow until the buyer confirms delivery. Every provider carries a visible trust signal before a buyer ever chats with them.

Verification stack

Phone, email, government ID, business registration, GST/NZBN, insurance, trade licence, background check, bank verification.

Escrow payment flow

Provider accepts → buyer pays into escrow → funds held → job completed → buyer releases payment → platform takes commission.

09

Differentiators

Nine mechanics that don't exist together in any single competitor today. Click to expand.

Reverse marketplace

+
The buyer posts once; every provider in range sees the same request and competes to win it — flipping the search burden onto supply.

Group buying

+
Many buyers with the same need (e.g. 100 households wanting solar) are pooled into one request that providers compete for as a batch, improving pricing for everyone.

Smart negotiation

+
Structured counter-offer flow — buyer proposes, provider counters, both accept — logged against the request instead of buried in chat.

Live countdown

+
Offers carry a visible expiry, creating urgency for the buyer to decide without pressuring the provider to lowball indefinitely.

Auto-invite

+
AI proactively invites the best-fit nearby providers to a request instead of waiting passively for discovery.

Recurring requests

+
Weekly cleaner, monthly lawns, yearly accountant — a request can auto-repost on a schedule, with the previous provider given first refusal.

Partial fulfilment

+
Bulk requests (500 chairs) can be split and fulfilled by multiple providers simultaneously, combined into one order.

Community trust score

+
A single composite score from reviews, verification, response time, completion rate, complaints and repeat-customer rate.

AI concierge

+
A single typed or spoken sentence — "paint my house next weekend, under $2,500" — is parsed into category, budget, dates, location and qualifications automatically.
10

v1.1 — fulfilment & trust additions

Benchmarked against on-demand home-services apps (e.g. Pronto). The reverse-marketplace loop stays the core model — these additions give commodity categories a faster lane, and give in-home jobs a stronger trust layer.

Speed

Instant · Scheduled · Recurring

A fulfilment-speed toggle on every request. Instant skips offer comparison and auto-matches the top-ranked available provider at a flat rate for commodity categories. Scheduled and Recurring keep the full compare-and-choose flow.

Basket

Task-stacking

Add multiple line items to one request — clean, laundry, ironing — so a single provider quotes and completes the whole basket in one visit instead of three separate postings.

Reliability

Auto-reassignment

If an accepted provider cancels or doesn't show, the system automatically re-offers the job to the next-best ranked provider from the original offer set — no restart for the buyer.

Policy

Tiered cancellation

Free cancellation up to a set window before the job; a small late-cancellation fee compensates the provider for a reserved slot. Shown at accept time, not buried in terms.

Trust

Servuber Verified sessions

Optional premium tier for in-home jobs: the provider's visit is recorded, encrypted and anonymised (faces blurred), accessible only if a dispute is raised, and deleted after a fixed retention window. Explicit two-stage consent — before payment, and again via OTP when the provider arrives.

Supply

Provider acquisition funnel

A dedicated "Become a Provider" onboarding path, plus a referral-based "Bring a Provider" program for existing providers to recruit others — separate from the buyer-side signup.

Consent for Servuber Verified is captured twice: once on a dedicated screen before payment, and again when the provider arrives, via the start-service OTP. Footage is never viewable by Servuber staff or the provider — only released for review by the buyer, and only in an active dispute.
